## 🧐 What Is cookietin?

cookietin is a friendly cookie manager and editor for your web browser. It works with **Firefox**, **Edge**, and **Chrome**. It shows you every single cookie your browser stores, including hidden ones from private windows, different containers, and partitioned cookies (a fancy way browsers separate data). If you've ever used an extension called "Cookie Quick Manager," think of cookietin as its smarter, modern replacement. It's built for regular people, not just tech experts.

Imagine cookies as little notes websites leave in your browser to remember you. Sometimes you want to see those notes, edit them, or delete them. cookietin gives you a simple dashboard to do exactly that. You can inspect any cookie, change its value, or remove it entirely. This helps you protect your privacy, fix login issues, or test websites like a developer would.

## 🎛️ How to Use cookietin (A Quick Tour)

Once you open cookietin, you'll see a clean interface. Here's what you can do:

- **Search Bar:** Type the name of a website (like `google.com`) to see all cookies for that site instantly.
- **Cookie List:** Every cookie is shown in a table with columns for Name, Value, Domain, Path, Expires, and Size. Scroll through or sort by clicking the column headers.
- **Edit a Cookie:** Click on any cookie row. A side panel will open where you can change the value, set a new expiration date, or modify the domain. Click "Save" to apply your changes.
- **Delete Cookies:** To remove a single cookie, click the trash icon next to it. To clear all cookies for a site, click the "Clear All" button at the top of the list for that domain.
- **View Cookie Details:** Hover over a cookie to see full technical information like SameSite attributes, whether it's HttpOnly or Secure, and which partition it belongs to.
